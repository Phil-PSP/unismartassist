📋 CSV Format Guide

Columns:

  • question_text - The question text (required)
  • question_type - multiple_choice, true_false, fill_blank, short_answer, essay
  • marks - Points for the question (default: 1)
  • order - Display order (default: 0)
  • options - Options separated by | (e.g., "A|B|C|D")
  • correct_answers - Indices of correct options (e.g., "1" or "0,2")
